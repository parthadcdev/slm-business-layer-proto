## Executive Summary

This comprehensive Business Requirements Document outlines the requirements for a next-generation, AI-powered Enterprise Business Platform that integrates inventory management, order fulfillment, customer relationship management, supplier partnerships, financial operations, business intelligence, and strategic decision-making capabilities into a unified, intelligent ecosystem.

## Comprehensive Business Rules

### BR-001: Dynamic Pricing and Promotions
**Rule**: Product pricing shall automatically adjust based on:
- Competitor pricing (within 5% band)
- Inventory levels (clearance pricing for slow-moving stock)
- Customer segment (loyalty tier discounts)
- Market demand (surge pricing for high-demand periods)
- Seasonal patterns (holiday and seasonal adjustments)

### BR-002: Intelligent Order Prioritization
**Rule**: Order processing priority shall be determined by:
1. Premium customer status and loyalty tier
2. Order value and profitability
3. Geographic proximity to fulfillment centers
4. Inventory availability and allocation
5. Shipping method and delivery commitments
6. Customer service level agreements

### BR-003: Automated Procurement Triggers
**Rule**: Purchase orders shall be automatically generated when:
- Inventory falls below dynamic reorder points
- Demand forecast indicates future stockout risk
- Supplier promotional pricing becomes available
- Quality issues trigger alternative supplier activation
- Contract terms require minimum purchase commitments

### BR-004: Advanced Supplier Performance Management
**Rule**: Supplier status shall be automatically updated based on:
- On-time delivery performance (target: 98%+)
- Quality metrics (defect rate <0.1%)
- Cost competitiveness (within market range)
- Financial stability (credit rating monitoring)
- Innovation and collaboration scores

### BR-005: Customer Lifecycle Management
**Rule**: Customer engagement strategies shall be triggered by:
- Purchase frequency changes (retention campaigns)
- Loyalty tier progression opportunities
- Product affinity shifts (cross-sell opportunities)
- Service interaction patterns (satisfaction monitoring)
- Competitive intelligence (retention strategies)

## Advanced Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: AI Query Processing Performance
**Given** a natural language business query is submitted,
**When** the AI processing engine receives the query,
**Then** the system shall return accurate results within 5 seconds for 95% of queries and provide query explanation and confidence scores.

### AC-002: Predictive Analytics Accuracy
**Given** the system generates demand forecasts,
**When** comparing predictions to actual demand,
**Then** the system shall achieve 85% accuracy for 30-day forecasts and 75% accuracy for 90-day forecasts.

### AC-003: Real-Time Decision Making
**Given** business conditions change (pricing, inventory, demand),
**When** the system detects these changes,
**Then** automated business decisions shall be executed within 2 minutes with audit trails and approval workflows for high-impact decisions.

### AC-004: Customer Experience Consistency
**Given** customer interactions across multiple channels,
**When** the customer engages through any touchpoint,
**Then** the system shall maintain consistent data and personalization with 99.9% accuracy across all channels.

### AC-005: Financial Data Integrity
**Given** financial transactions occur across the platform,
**When** financial reports are generated,
**Then** the system shall maintain 100% data accuracy with real-time reconciliation and automated variance investigation.
