# CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE AND ANALYTICS PLATFORM - BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

**Author:** Partha Chandramohan
**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** December 2024
**Document Type:** Specialized Business Requirements Document (BRD)
**Business Domain:** Customer Experience Management, Customer Analytics, and Relationship Intelligence

## Executive Summary

This BRD defines requirements for a comprehensive Customer Experience and Analytics Platform that transforms customer interactions into strategic business insights through AI-driven personalization, predictive analytics, and omnichannel experience optimization.

## Business Requirements

### BR-001: 360-Degree Customer Intelligence and Profiling
The system shall provide comprehensive customer intelligence including behavioral analytics, preference tracking, lifecycle management, and predictive customer insights across all touchpoints.

### BR-002: AI-Powered Personalization and Recommendation Engine
The system shall deliver personalized experiences through intelligent product recommendations, dynamic content personalization, and adaptive user interfaces based on customer behavior and preferences.

### BR-003: Omnichannel Customer Journey Orchestration
The system shall orchestrate seamless customer experiences across all channels including web, mobile, social media, email, and in-store interactions with consistent personalization and context preservation.

### BR-004: Predictive Customer Analytics and Insights
The system shall provide predictive analytics for customer behavior including churn prediction, lifetime value optimization, next-best-action recommendations, and market opportunity identification.

### BR-005: Customer Service Excellence and Automation
The system shall deliver exceptional customer service through AI-powered chatbots, automated issue resolution, intelligent case routing, and proactive customer support.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Customer Data Privacy and Consent Management
**Rule**: Customer data handling shall comply with:
- GDPR and CCPA privacy regulations
- Explicit consent management for data collection and processing
- Right to be forgotten and data portability requirements
- Transparent privacy policies and consent mechanisms
- Secure data handling and encryption standards

### BR-002: Customer Segmentation and Targeting Rules
**Rule**: Customer segmentation shall be based on:
- Behavioral patterns and engagement metrics
- Purchase history and transaction patterns
- Demographic and psychographic characteristics
- Customer lifecycle stage and journey position
- Value-based segmentation with lifetime value consideration

### BR-003: Personalization and Recommendation Logic
**Rule**: Personalization algorithms shall consider:
- Real-time customer behavior and context
- Historical purchase and interaction patterns
- Product affinity and category preferences
- Inventory availability and business objectives
- Ethical AI principles and bias prevention

### BR-004: Customer Communication Preferences
**Rule**: Customer communications shall respect:
- Channel preferences and opt-in/opt-out settings
- Frequency preferences and communication limits
- Content preferences and personalization settings
- Time zone and timing preferences
- Language and cultural considerations

### BR-005: Customer Service Level Agreements
**Rule**: Customer service performance shall meet:
- Response time SLAs by customer tier and channel
- Resolution time targets by issue complexity
- First contact resolution rate targets
- Customer satisfaction score minimums
- Escalation procedures and management notification

## Performance Metrics and KPIs

### Customer Experience Metrics
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Target NPS of 70+ with quarterly improvement tracking
- **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**: Maintain 95%+ customer satisfaction across all touchpoints
- **Customer Effort Score (CES)**: Minimize customer effort with target CES of 2.0 or lower
- **Customer Experience Index**: Composite customer experience score with benchmarking
- **Voice of Customer Score**: Automated sentiment analysis score with trend tracking

### Customer Retention and Growth Metrics
- **Customer Retention Rate**: Achieve 95%+ annual customer retention rate
- **Customer Churn Rate**: Maintain churn rate below 5% annually with early warning systems
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: Increase average CLV by 25% through optimization strategies
- **Customer Acquisition Cost**: Optimize CAC with target 3:1 CLV to CAC ratio
- **Revenue per Customer**: Increase average revenue per customer through upsell/cross-sell

### Customer Engagement Metrics
- **Engagement Score**: Dynamic customer engagement scoring with trend analysis
- **Channel Engagement**: Track engagement across all customer touchpoints
- **Content Engagement**: Monitor content engagement and personalization effectiveness
- **Campaign Effectiveness**: Measure marketing campaign performance and ROI
- **Customer Journey Completion**: Track customer journey completion rates and optimization

### Customer Service Metrics
- **First Contact Resolution**: Achieve 85%+ first contact resolution rate
- **Average Response Time**: Maintain under 2-hour response time for all channels
- **Service Quality Score**: Target 95%+ service quality score with continuous improvement
- **Agent Productivity**: Optimize agent productivity with AI-powered assistance
- **Customer Service ROI**: Measure customer service ROI and cost optimization
